The Technical Talks Series complements CyberColombia's hands-on schools and workshops with focused introductions to tools, architectures, scientific applications, and lessons from production systems.

## Topics

- High-performance and parallel programming.
- Scientific data workflows and reproducibility.
- AI/ML systems and emerging accelerators.
- Weather and climate applications.
- Quantum software and hybrid computing.

## Participate

Talks are announced through the [events hub](/events/) and CyberColombia's professional channels. Researchers and practitioners interested in contributing a session can contact [workshops@cybercolombia.org](mailto:workshops@cybercolombia.org).
