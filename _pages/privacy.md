---

CyberColombia uses aggregated website analytics to understand which public pages are useful to the community. The site also embeds selected content from third-party services such as LinkedIn, YouTube, and Google Sheets. Those providers may receive technical information when an embedded resource is loaded and apply their own privacy policies.

The public website does not sell personal information. Contact details voluntarily sent by email are used only to respond to the request or coordinate the relevant community activity.

Questions about this notice can be sent to [admin@cybercolombia.org](mailto:admin@cybercolombia.org).
