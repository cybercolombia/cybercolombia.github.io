## Books

### Mathematical and Physical Foundations for Quantum Computing

A university text by Esteban Hernández covering the foundations of qubits, quantum circuits, Hamiltonians, and Ising models, with worked examples and exercises. The fourth revised edition, version 09, is available in English and Spanish.

[English edition (PDF)](https://github.com/cybercolombia/cqa/blob/main/books/Mathematical_and_Physical_Foundations_for_Quantum_Computing_v09_EN.pdf) · [Edición en español (PDF)](https://github.com/cybercolombia/cqa/blob/main/books/Mathematical_and_Physical_Foundations_for_Quantum_Computing_v09_ES.pdf)

## Research manuscripts

### Beyond Classical Computer Science: Educating for Quantum Computing in Colombia

Examines the competencies needed for quantum computing and proposes an educational pathway in the Colombian context, connecting computing, physics, and the development of the quantum workforce.

[Read the manuscript (English PDF)](https://github.com/cybercolombia/cqa/blob/main/papers/Educating_for_Quantum_Computing_in_Colombia_EN.pdf)

### Evidence-Gated Admission Control for Autonomous Quantum Research: An Architecture and Simulation Study

Proposes a framework for conditioning access to quantum hardware on reproducible classical evidence. The study evaluates candidate readiness and resource allocation through simulation; its reported results do not establish validation on physical quantum hardware.

[Read the manuscript (English PDF, version 2)](https://github.com/cybercolombia/cqa/blob/main/papers/Evidence_Gated_Admission_Control_for_Autonomous_Quantum_Research_v2_EN.pdf)

### Exponential by Design: Why a Million Classical Cores Cannot Match a Hundred Good Qubits

Discusses classical parallelism, quantum interference, computational complexity, and the relationship between HPC and quantum computing. The comparison examines problem structure and hardware limitations rather than treating qubit counts as a general performance benchmark.

[Read the manuscript (English PDF)](https://github.com/cybercolombia/cqa/blob/main/papers/Exponential_by_Design_HPC_vs_Quantum_EN.pdf)

### From Acceleration to Feasibility: HPC–AI Convergence, Energy Constraints, and Public Policy for Technological Sovereignty in Colombia

Analyses the convergence of HPC and AI in Colombia, with attention to validation, infrastructure, energy requirements, and the capacity to sustain and evaluate advanced computing systems.

[Read the manuscript (English PDF, version 3.7)](https://github.com/cybercolombia/cqa/blob/main/papers/From_Acceleration_to_Feasibility_v3.7_EN.pdf)

## Reports

### Status of HPC in Colombia: A Vision from Centers

A CyberColombia report drawing on a survey of participating HPC centres. It describes their capabilities, collaboration, education, and operational challenges, providing context for the development of advanced computing in Colombia.

[Read the report (English PDF)](https://github.com/cybercolombia/cqa/blob/main/reports/Status_of_HPC_in_Colombia_2026_EN.pdf)

## Using and contributing resources

Consult each document for its authors, version, publication status, references, and reuse terms. When citing a work, identify the edition or version used. Copyright and reuse terms stated in each PDF apply to that document.

The repository is updated as further materials become available. To contribute a resource, suggest a correction, or discuss educational and research use, contact [quantum@cybercolombia.org](mailto:quantum@cybercolombia.org).
