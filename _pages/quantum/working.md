# From vision to action

CQA's 2026 program connects skills development, access to quantum platforms, regional collaboration, and application-led research. These activities form the first stage of the alliance's 2026–2036 roadmap.

## 2026 calendar

| Date | Activity | Location and format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **June 19, 2026** | [Quantum Day 2026](/events/quantum_day_2026/) — hands-on training with Amazon Braket, Xanadu, and PennyLane | Distributed through participating host universities |
| **October 21–23, 2026** | [Quantum Latino 2026](https://www.quantum-latino.com/) — the fourth edition of the regional quantum conference | Hybrid · Medellín, Colombia |
| **October 26–29, 2026** | [CILAMCE 2026](https://cilamce.com.br/) — including the first mini-symposium on *Methods in Quantum Computation and Quantum Information Processing* | Bogotá, Colombia |

## Current workstreams

### Talent and community

- Introductory and hands-on quantum computing training.
- Collaboration among educators, researchers, students, and industry practitioners.
- Connections between Colombia's university communities and the wider Quantum Latino network.

### Infrastructure and provider dialogues

CQA is holding technical discussions to understand available platforms, learning resources, and potential access pathways. Organizations engaged in these conversations include:

- NCSA
- IQM
- Xanadu
- Amazon Braket
- NVIDIA

These discussions should not be interpreted as formal partnerships unless an agreement is announced.

### Applied research

CQA is identifying use cases where quantum algorithms, simulation, and hybrid quantum-classical workflows can contribute to Colombian research and industry. Initial areas of interest include energy, finance, logistics, chemistry, materials, and biodiversity.

## Next milestones

- Formalize a shared alliance charter and participation model.
- Define a coordinated training pathway for students and educators.
- Map infrastructure access options for member institutions.
- Select initial application prototypes and industry collaborators.
- Establish baseline metrics for the first CQA ecosystem report.

## Take part

Researchers, educators, students, public institutions, technology providers, and companies are invited to contribute.
