CQA’s work grows through contributions from researchers, educators, professionals, and collaborating organisations. This page distinguishes completed activities, work in progress, and upcoming participation. Contributions are recognised alongside the universities, organisers, and individuals involved.

## Education and community: 2025–2026

### Quantum computing diploma programme · 2025

Members of the community that later formed CQA supported a quantum computing diploma programme with Universidad Distrital Francisco José de Caldas. The university’s 2025 report records the programme and 62 participants; the community’s contribution is documented in its own accounts and presentation materials.

[University report, page 32](https://planeacionud.udistrital.edu.co/sites/planeacion/files/archivos/2026-02/Informe%20inversion%202025.pdf).

### Q-AI Summer School · July 2025

The community supported the Q-AI Summer School at Universidad del Rosario, held on 14–18 July 2025. This educational experience forms part of the collaborative trajectory preceding CQA’s beginning in November 2025.

### Quantum Day · 19 June 2026

Quantum Day brought together participants from Amazon Braket and Xanadu/PennyLane for introductory sessions and practical exploration of quantum computing tools. The event was associated with the ninth Colombian HPC Summer School.

[Explore the event programme]({{ '/events/quantum_day_2026/' | relative_url }}).

### Engagement with Colombia’s technology community

Members of the alliance participated in the Bogotá Chamber of Commerce’s TechDay. Discussions with CINTEL and Cenisoft are part of ongoing efforts to connect scientific interests with the wider technology community.

## Scientific and technological exchange

Our reported exchanges include conversations with the NCSA team, Amazon Braket, Xanadu, IQM, D-Wave, Classiq, and NVIDIA, as well as discussions with Airbus about applications. These organisations contribute different perspectives on research, platforms, hardware, software, integration, and potential applications.

The purpose is to learn, compare approaches, and identify opportunities for cooperation. The scope of any infrastructure access or institutional commitment depends on the arrangements made by the participants concerned.

## Research and resources in progress

### coQuantum-research

An initiative developing an evidence-based computational co-researcher to propose and evaluate candidate approaches. The work remains in development; simulation results and validation on physical quantum hardware are distinct stages of investigation.

### Quantum simulation and application studies

Participants are exploring simulation, hybrid computational methods, and the formulation of problems in logistics and portfolio optimisation. Each study defines its scientific objectives, resource requirements, and appropriate classical comparisons.

### Educational resources and evaluation guidance

Work in progress includes resources on prerequisites for quantum computing and guidance for examining which parts of an algorithm may be suitable for quantum methods.

### Exchange through CILAMCE

The community reports support for the mini-symposium **Methods in Quantum Computation and Quantum Information Processing** within the XLVII Ibero-Latin American Congress on Computational Methods in Engineering, organised by Universidad del Rosario. Consult the [congress website](https://cilamce.com.br/) for the current programme and schedule.
